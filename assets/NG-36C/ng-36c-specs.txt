This are the specifications of a fictional console I'll
use as a guide for developing retro games

Fictional console specifications:

NG-36C (NES, Game Boy 36 Color)

SCREEN RESOLUTION: 240X240 px

GRID SIZE / TILE SIZE: 12x12 px

TILES ON SCREEN 20X20 tiles

TILE MODE: Simple Tile (1:1 mapping)

MASTER PALETTE: 35 Real Colors + 1 Transparent

PALETTES 2 X 8 palettes (8 for tiles and background, 8 for sprites)

COLORS PER PALETTE: 4 Real Colors
